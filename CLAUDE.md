## INVESTMENT PHILOSOPHY & EDGE

**Core thesis:** Find high-quality compounders trading at a discount to intrinsic value due to temporary misperception, narrative overhang, or macro noise — not structural deterioration.

**Edge sources:**

- Deep fundamental work on mid-cap names where sell-side coverage is thin
- Management credibility scoring vs. consensus expectations
- Filing-level diff analysis to catch early inflections before they're priced in
- Multi-scenario stress testing before sizing any position

**Time horizon:** 12–36 months. We are not traders. We size for conviction.

-----

## SECTOR COVERAGE UNIVERSE

**Primary coverage (deep diligence):**

- Technology: SaaS, cloud infrastructure, AI/ML picks-and-shovels
- Healthcare: MedTech, specialty pharma, health IT
- Consumer Discretionary: e-commerce enablement, premium brands
- Industrials: capital-light, high-ROIC businesses

**Opportunistic (must clear higher bar):**

- Financials, Energy, Materials — only on deep value thesis with clear catalyst

**Off limits:**

- Commodity pure-plays with no pricing power
- Pre-revenue biotech/clinical stage
- Highly leveraged real estate

-----

## DILIGENCE CHECKLIST (Must-Pass — ALL 6 Required)

- [ ] **1. Business Quality** — Durable competitive moat (pricing power, switching costs, network effects, or cost advantage). Rate 1–10. Must score ≥7.
- [ ] **2. Financial Quality** — Revenue quality (recurring vs. one-time), gross margin trend (stable or expanding), FCF conversion >50% of net income.
- [ ] **3. Management Credibility** — Score guidance vs. actuals for last 3 years. Must hit ≥70% of stated targets. Kill if credibility score <6/10.
- [ ] **4. Balance Sheet** — Net debt/EBITDA <3x OR net cash position. No covenant risk in next 18 months.
- [ ] **5. Variant Perception** — We must have a differentiated view vs. consensus. Articulate clearly: "The market thinks X. We think Y because Z."
- [ ] **6. Catalyst Timeline** — At least one identifiable catalyst within 12 months (earnings inflection, product launch, contract win, regulatory approval, buyback).

-----

## KILL CRITERIA (Walk away immediately)

- Insider selling >$10M in trailing 6 months (Form 4 crosscheck required)
- Revenue restatement or material weakness in internal controls
- Management guidance miss >3 consecutive quarters without explanation
- Customer concentration >40% in single customer without locked-in contract
- Debt covenant violation risk in next 12 months
- Thesis requires >40x NTM EBITDA multiple to work
- SEC comment letter unresolved >6 months

-----

## PREFERRED MULTIPLES & VALUATION APPROACH

**Growth names (>20% revenue growth):**

- Primary: EV/NTM Revenue (1–3x is cheap, 3–6x is fair, >8x needs exceptional quality)
- Secondary: Rule of 40 check (Revenue growth % + FCF margin % ≥ 40)
- DCF: 3-stage with terminal value; use WACC 9–11% for SaaS

**Value/compounders (10–20% growth):**

- Primary: EV/NTM EBITDA (8–12x cheap, 12–18x fair)
- Secondary: FCF yield (>5% is interesting, >8% is compelling)
- P/E for highly profitable names

**Cyclicals:**

- EV/Normalized EBITDA (trough-to-trough)
- Price/Book + ROE vs. historical

**Always include:** 52-week range context, implied upside to base/bull target, downside to bear case.

## THESIS STRUCTURE (Required format for every investment memo)

```
1. ONE-LINE THESIS
2. VARIANT PERCEPTION (What the market misunderstands)
3. BUSINESS OVERVIEW (What they do, how they make money)
4. COMPETITIVE MOAT (Source, durability, score)
5. FINANCIAL ANALYSIS (3-yr history + NTM estimates)
6. MANAGEMENT SCORECARD (Credibility score + evidence)
7. BULL CASE (Price target + assumptions)
8. BASE CASE (Price target + assumptions)
9. BEAR CASE (Price target + floor)
10. CATALYST TIMELINE (Specific events + dates)
11. RISK MATRIX (Top 5 risks, probability, impact, mitigation)
12. POSITION SIZING RECOMMENDATION (% of portfolio, entry range)
13. EXIT CRITERIA (What would make you sell)
```
